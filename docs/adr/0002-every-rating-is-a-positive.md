# Every Rating is a positive; Liked Songs and To Rip start at 3★

The listener's Verdicts are a Dislike (hates it, never again), a Pass (not for them, doesn't hate it) or a 1–5★ Rating, and every star count means they like the track: 1★ is "a little", not "bad". Discovery therefore treats every Rating as a positive weighted from light (1★) to heavy (5★), a Pass as a normal negative and a Dislike as the strongest negative. A track has one current Verdict; a newer one replaces the older, and the server keeps the history.

Liked Songs mirrors Ratings of 3★ and up, one way and only when a Verdict is given: a 3–5★ Rating adds the heart; a 1–2★ Rating, a Pass or a Dislike removes it; a heart added or removed in another Spotify client is never fought. An Unrated like counts as 3★ until the listener rates it. A Rating of 3★ or more on a track that isn't in the library sends it to To Rip. Any Verdict on a discovery takes it out of Discoveries at once.

**Consequences**: a reader who assumes 1★ means "bad" will get discovery's weights backwards. 1–2★ tracks exist only on the server, not in any Spotify client.
